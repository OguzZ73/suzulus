2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Süzülüş ("Gliding") is a relaxing, single-player casual arcade game. The player steers a hang glider pilot through changing skies and landscapes, avoids obstacles, rides air currents and collects feathers. It is aimed at casual players aged 9+ who want short, calm play sessions with atmospheric visuals and music. It works fully offline and needs no account.

3. HOW TO USE / ACCESS FEATURES
- No login or demo account is needed. Open the app and start a flight from the main menu.
- Use the two flight buttons on the right side of the screen: hold the upper one to climb, the lower one to dive; with neither held the glider sinks gently. Avoid obstacles; collect feathers.
- Every 1,200 m a portal appears; flying through it moves the player to a random new land (lakes, canyon, polar, volcano).
- Collected feathers are spent in the Shop: wings, scarves, pilots, and skill upgrades with a skill tree (Upgrades tab).
- Shop > Golden Feathers: 4 consumable In-App Purchases:
  com.suzulus.oyun.feathers300, com.suzulus.oyun.feathers750, com.suzulus.oyun.feathers1800, com.suzulus.oyun.feathers5000
  Golden Feathers are an in-game currency only; all content can also be earned by playing. A "Restore Purchases" button is in the Shop.
- Optional rewarded video ads (never forced): continue a flight once after crashing, double the feathers of a flight, or get a small amount of Golden Feathers in the Shop.
- Watch mode (main menu) shows a relaxing autopilot flight through empty skies, with no score.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple StoreKit 2: In-App Purchases.
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): optional rewarded video ads.
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): ad consent (GDPR) dialog; App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown before any personalised ads.
- Google Fonts: two web fonts for the UI text (falls back to system fonts offline).
No other backend, analytics, authentication or AI services are used. Game progress is stored only on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. The only difference is the legally required ad consent (UMP) dialog, which Google shows only in regions where it is required (e.g. EEA/UK). The game is available in English and Turkish: it follows the device language (Turkish devices get Turkish, all others English) and can be switched any time in Settings > Language.
